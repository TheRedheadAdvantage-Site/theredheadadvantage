The experience behind the advice

Built through experience, shaped by perspective.

I’ve spent my career working across strategy, finance, operations, ecommerce, and international growth. Along the way, I’ve seen how businesses make decisions, how competing priorities get managed, and what it takes to turn a plan into something real.

The work taught me a lot about how organizations grow. The harder seasons taught me just as much about leading through uncertainty, rethinking what success looks like, and finding a way forward when the original plan changes.

The Redhead Advantage is where I share both sides of that experience: the professional lessons and the more personal ones, in the practical and honest way I wish someone had shared them with me earlier.
