Accessibility Statement

Last updated: August 4, 2026

The Redhead Advantage LLC is committed to making its website usable and accessible to a broad audience.

Our efforts

We aim to follow recognized accessibility practices, including semantic structure, visible keyboard focus, keyboard-accessible navigation, readable contrast, descriptive alternative text, labeled forms, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support. Accessibility is an ongoing effort, and no statement or automated test can guarantee that every page, device, assistive technology, or third-party service is fully accessible.

Feedback and assistance

If you encounter an accessibility barrier or need content in another format, email hello@theredheadadvantage.com and describe the page, issue, and assistance requested. We will make reasonable efforts to respond and provide access.
