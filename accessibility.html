Accessibility Statement

Last updated: August 4, 2026

The Redhead Advantage LLC is committed to making its website usable and accessible to a broad audience.

Our efforts

We aim to use clear structure, readable contrast, keyboard-accessible navigation, descriptive alternative text, labeled forms, and responsive layouts. Accessibility is an ongoing effort, and some content or third-party services may not yet meet every user’s needs.

Feedback and assistance

If you encounter an accessibility barrier or need content in another format, email hello@theredheadadvantage.com and describe the page, issue, and assistance requested. We will make reasonable efforts to respond and provide access.
