What’s inside

Turn “what do I need to do?” into a real plan.

The planner helps you move beyond vague promotion advice and prepare for a conversation grounded in role expectations, evidence, decision-making, and next steps.

  • Clarify the title or level you’re working toward and what actually changes at that level.
  • Identify what your manager would need to see and where genuine gaps still exist.
  • Understand who recommends and approves promotions, plus timing and business constraints.
  • Build your evidence across scope, business impact, leadership, ownership, and influence.
  • Prepare the questions you want to ask and capture exactly what you agree to do next.
